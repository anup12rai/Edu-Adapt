About EduAdapt

A smarter approach to academic planning

EduAdapt is designed as a web-based academic planning and performance analysis system for students who need more than a static to-do list.

The platform combines syllabus management, adaptive study planning, task tracking, study-session logging, analytics, academic risk identification, and personalized recommendations into a single system.

Whether you are following a formal academic curriculum or working toward your own learning goals, EduAdapt helps turn your available time and academic workload into a structured study process.
